      How it works, and privacy

      Each device opens this page. One device shows a code, and the others join it with the QR code or by typing the code. Once paired, files travel directly between the devices over an encrypted WebRTC connection. On the same Wi-Fi they stay on your local network and never touch the internet.

      The free PeerJS service only helps the devices find each other and never sees your files. If the devices aren't on the same network, files still go directly over the internet where possible. Some office and school networks block direct connections; then files go through a relay and are slower. Each device's card shows which route it's using.

      Received files stay in this tab until you save or clear them, or close the page. Large files are kept in your browser's private storage while they arrive, so there's no size limit beyond your free disk space. Keep the page open on both devices until transfers finish.
