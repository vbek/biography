Vitrine

Free digital signage that runs on your own PC. Upload images and videos, build playlists, schedule them, and show them on any screen with a browser.

Download for Windows

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit

Available for Windows PCs only. You can't install it on a phone, Mac or Linux computer.

Get started

  1. Download the zip file.
  2. Extract it. Right-click the file and choose Extract All.
  3. Open "Start Vitrine" in the extracted folder. If Windows SmartScreen appears, choose More info, then Run anyway. It appears because this free app isn't code-signed.
  4. Create your owner account in the browser page that opens. Keep the black window open while you use Vitrine.

Your data stays on your PC

The database and uploads are stored on your computer. This website never receives them. Vitrine contacts other services only for content you add, such as web pages, YouTube, news feeds and weather.

Use your phone over Wi-Fi

In Vitrine, open Screens and choose Add a screen. Scan the QR code with a phone or tablet, or open the address shown in a TV's browser. Devices must be on the same Wi-Fi, and the PC needs to stay on.

Every browser sees the same data

Chrome, Edge and Firefox on your PC all open the same local app, so your playlists and screens are the same in each.

Good to know

  • Network: if Windows asks, allow Vitrine on private networks so other devices can reach it. Connections on your network aren't encrypted, so use it only on networks you trust.
  • Update: close Vitrine, delete the old folder, and extract the new version. Your data is kept.
  • Uninstall: delete the folder. To remove your data too, delete %LOCALAPPDATA%\Vitrine.
