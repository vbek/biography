Sidescreen

Use a tablet, phone or spare laptop as a screen for your Windows PC or Mac. Open this page on both devices. Nothing to install.

This browser can't share its screen, so this device can only be used as a screen. To share, open this page on a Windows PC or Mac in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.

How it works

  1. On your computer, choose Share this computer and pick a screen or window.
  2. On the tablet or phone, scan the QR code or type the 6-letter code.
  3. Tap the screen for full screen, fit or fill, sound and connection details.
Want a real extended display?

A browser can show an existing screen or window, but it can't add a new monitor to your computer. Two ways around that:

  • Share one window. Share the app you want on the side, such as notes, chat or a reference PDF, and keep working on your main screen.
  • Add a virtual monitor first. A virtual display driver (Windows) or a virtual screen app (macOS) adds an empty monitor that you can arrange like a real one. Share that screen here and the tablet becomes a true extended display. These are third-party apps, so check that you trust one before you install it.

Taps on the tablet don't control the computer. Use the computer's own mouse and keyboard.

Privacy and connection

The picture travels directly between your devices over an encrypted WebRTC connection. The free PeerJS service only helps the two devices find each other and never sees the stream. For the lowest delay, keep both devices on the same Wi-Fi. Some office and school networks block direct connections.
