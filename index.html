Instance-Level Bacteria Detection and Segmentation

Deep learning for early detection of E. coli and Salmonella in time-stamped microscopy images, on plain agar and on an onion food matrix. A background-aware Mask R-CNN and FPN pipeline detects both species after 2 hours of incubation, against 18–24 hours for a plate count, at mAP@0.5 of about 0.95 and mIoU of 0.91–0.98. Published at IEEE ICMLA 2025 (oral) and extended in my M.S. thesis, where a compact ResNet-based model matched larger SAM and DINOv3 models at 8.1 ms per image, 22× faster than the SAM-based model.

On-Device AI Tools

A free suite of tools with no accounts and no uploads, because the work happens on your own device. LocalLabel annotates images and video for machine learning, with AI pre-labeling from any ONNX model. Document Reader reads files aloud offline in up to 31 languages. Media Studio edits images and video and makes Whisper subtitles. There is also a PDF Editor, a receipt-scanning Financial Planner and Vitrine, digital signage for your own PC. Built with ONNX Runtime Web, WebGPU, WebAssembly and Transformers.js.

Browser Games

Five free games that run in the browser on a phone or computer, with no sign-up. AR Survival is steered by your head through real-time MediaPipe face tracking. SeekBot - Precision Retrieval AI

A full-stack RAG chatbot that lets you chat with your own documents. Upload PDFs, Word files, CSVs, or paste a URL - SeekBot indexes your content into a local FAISS vector store and answers questions using a 10-node LangGraph pipeline backed by HuggingFace LLMs (Llama 3.1 8B / Qwen 2.5 72B). Fully local embeddings, persistent sessions, and real-time model switching.
