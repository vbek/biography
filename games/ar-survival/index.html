How to play
  • Camera: move your head to dodge. A red ! at the top of the screen and a dashed line show where something will fall.
  • Missiles: you carry 3 and they reload over time. Each one homes in on its target, and warships and warplanes take two hits.
  • Laptop: Space fires at the target in the green brackets, or click any object. ←↑↓→ or WASD move, Q/E push, P pause, M sound, F fullscreen.
  • Phone: tap an object or the FIRE button to shoot, drag to move, and use the arrow buttons to push.
  • Scoring: survive, dodge, destroy and push. Unbroken streaks raise your multiplier up to ×5.

Face tracking runs only on this device. Camera video is never uploaded or stored. The AI files download once, then load from your browser's cache.
